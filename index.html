Intro

At NVTech Consulting, we help small and medium businesses work smarter through simple, practical technology solutions. Many business owners still rely on outdated tools or manual processes, not because they want to — but because better options feel out of reach. Whether you’re unsure where to begin or overwhelmed by choices, we’re here to simplify the path.

We take a clear, no-fluff approach: first, we understand your current workflows; then, we suggest ways to improve them using tools like automation, AI, and cost-effective platforms such as Trello, Zapier, Airtable, or Google Workspace. Our goal? Help you save time, cut unnecessary costs, and focus more on growing your business — not drowning in operations.

We also build our own tools. Our flagship is GearvOS, modular business software for SMEs. See what we're building, or contact us to talk about your business.

Prototype

TopicRelay — a workflow dashboard for Trimble Connect

A working prototype that turns raw issue-tracking data from Trimble Connect (a project collaboration platform used in construction and design) into a clear dashboard — status and priority breakdowns, on-time delivery tracking, one-click Excel exports. About

NVTech Consulting started the way most of our clients' problems did: from firsthand frustration. Before NVTech, we ran and worked inside small businesses ourselves — enough to feel exactly where outdated tools, manual spreadsheets, and disconnected systems quietly eat into a day and a bottom line.

That experience shapes two things we do. We work directly with SMEs to understand how they actually operate, then introduce automation, AI, and the right low-cost tools — without assuming every business needs the same fix. Even the smallest adjustment can make a big impact on the bottom line.

Because we kept running into the same gaps across different industries, we started building our own software. GearvOS, our modular business platform, and prototypes like TopicRelay came directly out of problems we saw firsthand — not out of a lab.

Our approach stays the same either way: no tech jargon, no fluff, and no unnecessary complexity. Just honest guidance and tools built to actually get used.
