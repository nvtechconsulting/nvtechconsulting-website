About

NVTech Consulting started from firsthand frustration — before this, we ran and worked inside small businesses ourselves, and felt exactly where outdated tools and disconnected systems quietly eat into a day and a bottom line. That's why we work directly with SMEs to understand how they actually operate, then bring in automation, AI, and the right low-cost tools — because even the smallest adjustment can make a big impact on the bottom line.

Because we kept hitting the same gaps across industries, we started building our own software. GearvOS, and prototypes like TopicRelay, came directly out of problems we saw firsthand — not out of a lab. Same principle either way: no tech jargon, no fluff, no unnecessary complexity — just honest guidance and tools people actually use.
